# Model card template

Status: TEMPLATE - no trained or evaluated model is represented here.

## Intended use and exclusions

Define the users, decision, prediction target, horizon, and situations where the model must not be used.

## Data and representativeness

Record source, licence, geography/domain, period, sampling, exclusions, feature availability, target construction, missing data, and known transfer limits.

## Model and training

Document preprocessing, algorithm, baseline, tuning, calibration, training boundary, configuration, and input/model identities.

## Evaluation

Report held-out results, uncertainty, subgroup/error support counts, calibration, and robustness. State what was and was not tested.

## Decision assumptions

List capacity/cost assumptions and any outcome or treatment evidence needed. Avoid a business-uplift claim unsupported by the dataset.

## Risks and maintenance

Document failure cases, out-of-distribution behavior, fairness/selection concerns, and what evidence would trigger review or retraining.
