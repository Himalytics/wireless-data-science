# Results report template

Status: TEMPLATE - analysis has not been executed.

## Question and decision

State the product question, unit of analysis, target, intended decision, and which claims the dataset can support.

## Source and study population

Identify the observed source, licence, input identity, period, exclusions, sampling/selection effects, missingness, and domain limitations.

## Hypotheses and design

Separate prespecified hypotheses from exploratory findings. Explain split design, baseline, preprocessing/tuning boundaries, models, metrics, and uncertainty assumptions.

## Measured results

Populate only after execution. Include baseline/model comparison, uncertainty, support counts, and figures linked to saved chart data. An inconclusive or negative finding must remain visible.

## Robustness and errors

Report ablations, subgroup/error analysis, sensitivity to reasonable design changes, and failure cases. Describe how these affect the interpretation.

## Interpretation and decision

Separate observed findings, assumptions, recommendations, and additional evidence required. Do not turn predictive associations into causes or risk predictions into treatment effects.

## Reproduction and limits

Record executed commands, environment, seeds/configuration, split/input identities, output locations, unresolved limitations, and the next experiment.
