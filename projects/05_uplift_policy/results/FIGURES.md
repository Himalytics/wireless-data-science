# Exact scientific figure provenance

All five PNGs are generated by `run.py::figures()` from saved CSVs, never by image synthesis. Scope: Criteo CRITEO-UPLIFT v2.1, verified 2026-11-23, held-out grouped sample **n=210,123**; no event dates are available. Dataset identity and SHA256: `source.json`. Attribution: Criteo AI Lab; Diemert, Betlei, Renaudin & Amini (2018), *A Large Scale Benchmark for Uplift Modeling*. Adapted tables and figures: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/. Noncommercial method study; no original-population or telecom effect claim.

| Figure | Exact table and columns | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 01_arm_rates.png | `audit.csv`: split=test, treatment, n, visits, rate | Observed marginal visit percentages, not estimated individual effects; arm counts annotated |
| 02_capacity.png | `capacity.csv`: capacity, policy, value, low, high, n | Frozen ranking at hypothetical capacities; IPW contrast per 1,000 eligible; random baseline |
| 03_policy.png | `policies.csv`: policy, incremental_per_1000, low, high | Same 20% capacity, three policies; support and observed arm/event counts in source table |
| 04_deciles.png | `deciles.csv`: decile, n, treated, control, mean_predicted_per_1000, observed_ipw_per_1000, low, high | Exploratory predicted versus observed contrasts, within-decile denominator |
| 05_sensitivity.png | `sensitivity.csv`: estimator, advantage_over_random, low, high | Same frozen 20% policy, alternative evaluation scores |

Intervals: 500 profile-cluster Poisson multiplier replicates, 95% conditional percentile, fixed fits/ranking/propensity, pointwise rather than simultaneous. They exclude training, selection, privacy sampling and unknown advertiser-cluster uncertainty. Raw holdout contributions and assignments are in `predictions.csv.gz`; paired primary comparisons in `paired_comparisons.csv`, DR comparisons in `dr_comparisons.csv`. Table rounding in prose does not replace these exact data.
