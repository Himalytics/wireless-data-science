# Product investigation brief

Use public complaint signals to choose questions for research, not to rank carriers or infer churn.

Validated 29,932 real source records for [2025-09-01, 2025-10-01).
Published warehouse: 29,932 records; 17,280 classified as wireless Phone/Internet complaints.

| Issue | Latest complete week | Previous complete week | Change | Action |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Unwanted Calls | 2017 | 2060 | -43 | Review high-volume issue |
| Billing | 1041 | 925 | 116 | Investigate increase; validate with independent evidence |
| Equipment | 367 | 349 | 18 | Investigate increase; validate with independent evidence |
| Availability (including rural call completion) | 291 | 267 | 24 | Investigate increase; validate with independent evidence |
| Number Portability (keeping your number if you change providers) | 184 | 145 | 39 | Investigate increase; validate with independent evidence |

Decision: investigate high-volume issues and sustained increases with product/support research. Treat these as hypotheses.
Measure investigation yield and time-to-detection first. A product intervention needs a separate randomized or otherwise justified evaluation with exposure denominators, guardrails, and actual retention outcomes.

Partial weeks are excluded from the priority comparison. Missing previous-period issue counts become zero only when all seven days were ingested. No claim of real business uplift is made.
